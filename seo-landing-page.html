Planning Checklist

Know What to Expect Before the Party

Clear preparation makes the day easier for parents, guests, and our venue team. We recommend following these steps when organising your celebration.

  1. 01

    Select Your Party Package

    Choose between the Climb Party and Climb and Cave Party according to your child's age and preferred activities. If you are uncertain, contact our party team to discuss the options.

  2. 02

    Reserve Enough Places

    Book for the number of children you intend to invite so their places are secured. Party bookings require a minimum of 10 participants.

  3. 03

    Pay the Booking Deposit

    A 50% non-refundable deposit is required to secure a party booking.

  4. 04

    Send the Party Invitations

    Once your party is booked, send the invitations and begin collecting responses from your guests.

  5. 05

    Confirm the Final Details

    Confirm final attendance numbers and relevant booking information seven days before the celebration. Numbers can be reduced to the minimum group size up to one week before the event.

  6. 06

    Prepare Participants for the Day

    Guests should wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing and runners. Footwear is required for all climbers. Every participant also needs a valid waiver.

  7. 07

    Arrive Early

    We recommend arriving 10 to 15 minutes before the scheduled start so participants are ready for the safety briefing and harness fitting.

  8. 08

    Enjoy the Celebration

    Once the formalities are complete, the children can start climbing, exploring, and encouraging their friends.

FAQs

Get Answers You Need Before Choosing Our Rock Climbing Birthday Parties

(03) 9558 1199
1. What is included in a rock climbing birthday party at Activate by Hardrock?

The inclusions depend on the package you select. Our Climb Party includes a 15-minute safety briefing and harness fitting, one hour of access to our Clip 'n Climb arena, and 30 minutes in a private party room. You can also choose from the available food packages.

Our Climb and Cave Party includes the safety briefing and harness fitting, one hour of climbing and artificial caving, 30 minutes of private party room access, and a deluxe food package. Party bookings are available for groups of 10 to 45 participants.

2. What ages are suitable for a rock climbing party?

Our Climb Party is suitable for children aged five and over. The Climb and Cave Party is suitable for children aged eight and over because it includes access to the artificial caving activity.

When selecting a package, consider the birthday child's age and the ages of the invited guests. If the group includes children of different ages, contact us before booking so we can help you understand which option may be most appropriate.

3. Do children need climbing experience to attend a party?

No previous climbing experience is necessary. Our Clip 'n Climb arena features more than 30 challenges with varying difficulty levels, allowing beginners and confident young climbers to select challenges that suit them.

Participants receive a safety briefing and harness fitting before activity time begins. Children can then explore the arena, try different climbs, and progress according to their confidence and ability.

4. Why choose Activate by Hardrock as a kids birthday party venue Melbourne families can enjoy?

Our Heatherton venue combines adventurous indoor activities, private party room access, food options, and practical booking support. This saves families from having to coordinate entertainment, a party space, and food across several locations or suppliers.

Climbing also gives guests an active experience that encourages confidence, movement, problem-solving, and friendly encouragement. With options for children aged five to 16 and group sizes from 10 to 45 participants, you can select a package suited to your celebration.

5. How long does a rock climbing birthday party run?

Both packages begin with a 15-minute safety briefing and harness fitting. Guests then receive one hour of activity time, followed by 30 minutes in a private party room.

We recommend asking guests to arrive 10 to 15 minutes early. This gives the group time to assemble and prepare before the scheduled briefing. Arriving late may reduce the time available for the party experience.

6. What should children wear to the party?

Participants should wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows them to climb and move easily. Runners or other suitable enclosed footwear should also be worn, as climbers must wear footwear.

Avoid clothing or accessories that could interfere with the harness or climbing activities. Include clothing and footwear guidance on the party invitations so parents can prepare their children before arriving.

7. Can you accommodate dietary requirements?

Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and lactose-free options may be available. If a guest has allergies or specific dietary needs, provide the details when confirming your booking.

For Kosher, Halal, or other specialised requirements, please contact our team directly. Early notice gives us the best opportunity to discuss the available arrangements and help you make an informed decision.

8. Are parents required to supervise children during the party?

Children under 12 must be supervised by a responsible adult while participating in the activities. Parents and supervising adults must also follow the instructions provided by our venue team.

Make sure the invited families understand any supervision requirements before the event. If you have questions about how supervision applies to your group, contact us before booking.

9. Can I bring a birthday cake, decorations, or party bags?

Yes. Families can bring a birthday cake, party bags, and themed decorations to personalise the celebration. Piñatas and party poppers are not permitted.

The private party room becomes available during the allocated room period, not during the climbing session. Speak with our team before the event if you have questions about decorations, setup requirements, or suitable items to bring.

10. What information should I provide when choosing a rock climbing birthday party?

Tell us the birthday child's age, your preferred date, the estimated number of participants, and whether you are interested in the Climb Party or Climb and Cave Party. You should also provide details about dietary requirements, allergies, and any questions relating to your guests.

Accurate information helps us guide you towards the most suitable package and prepare for your group. Always book enough places for the children you plan to invite, then confirm your final numbers and booking details at least seven days before the party.
