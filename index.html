FAQs

Good to know

Quick answers to the questions we hear most. Can't find what you're after? Our team is happy to help.

Call (03) 9558 1199
What age can climb?

Clip 'n Climb is recommended for ages 5 and up. Adults are very welcome to climb too, and our team will help every climber find challenges that suit their ability.

Do I need to book in advance?

We recommend booking online to secure your preferred session time, especially on weekends and during school holidays.

Do I need to sign a waiver?

Yes. Every climber needs a signed waiver. Save time on the day by completing it online before you arrive.

What should we wear?

Comfortable clothing you can move in and enclosed shoes are best. Our team will fit your harness before you start.

Can parents watch?

Absolutely. Grab a coffee from the cafe and watch the action from the viewing areas.

Do you host groups and schools?

Yes. We host birthday parties, school excursions, scout groups and social groups. Send us an enquiry and we will tailor a session for you.
